Flight Alerts (Chris) - SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Program name and description

Flight Alerts (Chris) is an SMS test program operated by Chris. It sends test flight-arrival check-in messages to a small group of colleagues who have verbally agreed to take part in testing. When a test flight is past its expected arrival, you may receive a message asking whether you have landed. You can reply Y if landed, N if not landed, or with your estimated minutes until arrival. You may also receive a test escalation message and a confirmation that your reply was recorded. All messages are labeled TEST.

Message frequency

Up to 5 messages per test session.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opt-out

Reply HELP for help.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

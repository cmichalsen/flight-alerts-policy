Flight Alerts (Chris) - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Chris ("we", "us"), operator of the Flight Alerts (Chris) SMS test program, collects and uses information from people who agree to receive Flight Alerts (Chris) text messages.

About the program

Flight Alerts (Chris) is a small test program run by Chris. It sends test flight-arrival check-in text messages to a limited group of colleagues who have verbally agreed to take part in testing. Messages ask whether a flight has landed and accept replies of Y, N, or an estimated number of minutes until arrival.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, which you provide when you agree to take part.
  • The content of your text message replies (for example Y, N, or an estimated number of minutes), and the date and time they were sent.
  • Message delivery status provided by our SMS provider.

How we use your information

We use this information only to send you Flight Alerts (Chris) test messages, to record your replies, to respond to HELP and STOP requests, and to test and improve the flight-arrival check-in system.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your phone number and messages are processed by our SMS service provider (Twilio) solely to deliver messages and receive replies. We do not otherwise share your information except where required by law.

Opting out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help.
